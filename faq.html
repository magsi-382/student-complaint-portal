Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions.

1. What is a student complaint?

It is a concern raised by a student about an institute-related issue.

2. What can I complain about?

Possible topics include academic matters, administration, facilities, and student services.

3. How should I write a complaint?

Explain the facts clearly and use respectful language.

4. Can I track my complaint here?

No. This is a static educational website and has no tracking system.

5. Does the website store my information?

No. The demonstration forms do not store or send information.

6. Where should I submit a real complaint?

Contact your institute's appropriate office and follow its official process.
