How It Works

Understand the basic complaint process.

  1. Identify the Issue

    Clearly identify the concern you want to raise.

  2. Prepare the Details

    Write the facts, dates, and relevant information.

  3. Contact the Relevant Office

    Use your institute's official complaint procedure.

  4. Follow Up

    Keep any reference information and ask the relevant office about the next steps.

This page describes a general process, not a live complaint-tracking service.
