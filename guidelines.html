Complaint Guidelines

Tips for communicating your concerns clearly.

Before Writing a Complaint

  • Explain the issue clearly.
  • Include relevant dates and facts.
  • Use polite and respectful language.
  • Include only information relevant to the issue.
  • Avoid false or unsupported accusations.
  • Do not share private information unnecessarily.

After Preparing Your Complaint

Review your description, check the details, and follow your institute's official procedure for submitting it.
